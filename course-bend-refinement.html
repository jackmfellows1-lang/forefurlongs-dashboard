ForeFurlongs Brand Studio | Course Bend Refinement

A mark with movement.

The Course Bend direction is distinctive because it suggests more than one thing: the geometry of a racecourse, momentum around a turn and the idea that reading position matters. These refinements test how simple the final mark should be.

Variation 1

Essential Bend

The original idea, tightened. Two racing lines and one gold marker. The best balance of clarity and character.

Variation 2

Open Track

A circular version for social avatars and membership badges. Softer, but slightly more sports-club in character.

Variation 3

Pure Bend

The most minimal version. Strongest as a favicon, although it loses a little of the racecourse depth.
