ForeFurlongs Brand Studio | Course Bend Study

Six ways around the bend.

The course-bend idea can carry different personalities. These alternatives range from a clean editorial symbol to a sharper racing-line mark, while keeping the modern `FOREFURLONGS` wordmark unchanged.

A

Classic Bend

Balanced and recognisable. The current front runner, with a clear two-rail course shape.

B

Sharper Turn

A more tactical corner. It feels faster and more sporting, with a stronger sense of positioning.

C

Club Roundel

The circular version. Naturally suited to social avatars, badges and future membership treatments.

D

Three Rails

A more detailed course treatment. Slightly richer and more analytical, but still calm.

E

Racing Line

The boldest version. A subtle directional notch adds the idea of reading the optimal route.

F

Pure Route

The most minimal symbol. A single route between two points, with excellent favicon clarity.
