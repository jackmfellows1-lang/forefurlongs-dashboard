Keep the identity. Simplify the symbol.

The approved modern wordmark remains unchanged. These emblem options remove the gold-line double-`F` treatment and focus on a single memorable racing cue that can work in the navigation, on social posts and as a favicon.

Option A

Course Bend

A minimal racecourse curve and one gold marker. Distinctive, calm and not reliant on letters.

Option B

Furlong Post

The clearest racing reference. A finishing-post rhythm beside a single modern `F`, with no double-monogram clutter.

Option C

Stride Signal

An abstract racing-stride pulse. This is the boldest challenger-brand option and works well for social media.

Option D

Four Marker

A compact furlong-marker badge. It gives the brand a more unusual, ownable reference to the name.
