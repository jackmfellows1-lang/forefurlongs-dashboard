Different ideas. Same confidence.

The modern wordmark is still doing its job. This sheet leaves the course-bend family behind and tests six new emblem personalities: racing silks, a horse-line mark, a furlong numeral, a photo-finish motif, an editorial seal and a racecard-inspired symbol.

A

Silks Diamond

A modern interpretation of racing silks. Bold, recognisable and naturally suited to app icons and merchandise.

B

Thoroughbred Line

A restrained horse-head line mark. More emotive and unmistakably racing, while avoiding a generic silhouette.

C

Four Furlongs

An ownable link to the brand name. The numeral makes a strong social badge and has a challenger-brand edge.

D

Photo Finish

Race-line movement through a photo-finish grid. This has a sharper intelligence feel and a memorable visual rhythm.

E

Intelligence Seal

A simplified members-club roundel. More established and sponsor-ready, but still clean enough for digital use.

F

Racecard Edge

A racecard grid with one clear route through the data. Most explicit about your product’s analytical advantage.
