ForeFurlongs Brand Studio | Final Shortlist

Modern wordmark. Racing character.

The stronger typography from Concept D is now the foundation. These three routes differ mainly in how much racing symbolism the emblem carries.

Option 1

The Rail Mark

The cleanest evolution of D. The double-F emblem uses racing-rail stripes without feeling decorative or old fashioned.

Option 2

The Post Mark

A finishing post beside a modern `F`. More explicitly racing-led while keeping the same confident wordmark.

Option 3

The Course Mark

A slightly softer emblem with a curved course line. Strong for social posts, but less minimal than the first two.
