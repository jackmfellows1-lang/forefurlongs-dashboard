ForeFurlongs Brand Studio

Logo directions

Four clean vector concepts for the ForeFurlongs identity. Each is designed to stay recognisable in the website header, on social-media graphics and at small favicon size.

Concept A

Finish Line

A sharp `FF` monogram with a subtle finishing-post rhythm. Clear, modern and easy to recognise at small sizes.

Concept B

Track Loop

A distinctive course-outline emblem with a single `F`. Slightly more editorial and closely tied to racing.

Concept C

Signal Mark

An `F` paired with rising data bars. The clearest expression of racing form interpreted through intelligence.

Concept D

Racing Stripe

A premium double-`F` badge with a gold baseline. The most established, members-club direction.
