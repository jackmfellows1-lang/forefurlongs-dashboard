ForeFurlongs Brand Studio | Round Two

More racing. More identity.

This round moves away from generic analytics icons. The marks are quieter, more distinctive and closer to the kind of identity a serious racing subscriber product could own for years.

Concept A

The Editorial Wordmark

Elegant and confident. The small finishing-post device gives it a racing signature without turning the logo into an illustration.

Concept B

The Racing Club Crest

The most established direction. A refined seal for membership, social content and future race sponsorship without feeling old fashioned.

Concept C

The Furlong Marker

A unique racing reference with a more contemporary wordmark. Strongest if you want the brand name to feel ownable and unmistakable.

Concept D

The Silk Stripe

A modern emblem inspired by racing silks and course rails. Bolder and more energetic, but still suitable for a premium product.

My instinct from this round: Concept A is the most premium website brand; Concept B is the strongest membership badge; Concept C is the most distinctive racing identity. We can also combine the wordmark from A with the crest from B.
